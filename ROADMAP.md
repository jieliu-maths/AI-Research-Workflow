# Roadmap / 路线图

The public release is AI4Math-first. AI4S is experimental. Planned integrations
are not included merely because their evidence contract is documented.

## Implemented in 2.7.0

- Portable English/Chinese entry points and role prompts; MIT licensing.
- Dated primary-source landscape review with explicit comparison limitations.
- Evidence-bundle metadata/content inspection for external tools, without
  command replay, proof checking, or automatic claim promotion.
- Offline bounded-arithmetic and synthetic held-out experiment examples.
- Separate fresh-directory AI4S scaffold and scientific experiment protocol.
- Source-publication checks and macOS/Linux regression CI configuration.
- Existing AI4Math claim graph, route review, human gates, dashboard, reports,
  transactional installation, and conflict-preserving upgrades remain in place.

## Next: evidence adapters and onboarding

- A Lean adapter that pins toolchains and dependencies, replays builds, inventories
  axioms/placeholders, and returns artifacts for statement-mapping review.
- A symbolic-computation adapter with domain/assumption tracking and exact versus
  numerical result classes; a literature adapter retaining search logs and
  cited theorem passages. No new service becomes a source of canonical truth.
- Bounded context/handoff packets and dependency-version freshness, evaluated
  against the existing QED and MathResearch Ledger designs before adding an MCP adapter.
- Reviewed small mathematical examples, an onboarding checklist, and measured
  human review effort. Generic host adapters must preserve independent tasks
  and owner gates before being described as supported.
- Bounded execution, cancellation, and actual resource receipts for adapters.
  A manually declared budget is not automatic enforcement.

## Later: validate AI4S and measure effectiveness

- Scientific claim/evidence schemas, independently repeated experiments,
  uncertainty/robustness fixtures, and reviewed data-access adapters.
- Managed AI4S migration and upgrades only after their semantics are tested.
- Predeclared comparisons against single-chat and lightweight multi-role workflows
  under matched budgets, with blind external assessment and disclosed failures.
  See the [evaluation proposal](docs/landscape-review.en.md#evaluation-proposal-not-a-completed-benchmark).
