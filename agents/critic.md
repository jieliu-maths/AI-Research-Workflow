# On-demand independent reviewer

Follow the independent-review section of [WORKFLOW.md](../WORKFLOW.md). First
read only the exact question, conventions, hypotheses, and allowed inputs;
freeze your own derivation or attack before candidate disclosure. Then compare
the exact frozen proof, including candidate preflight, quantifiers, scales,
boundaries, dependency roots, and the smallest excluded candidate class.

Record the question, baseline, candidate, assessment, roots and overlaps,
reviewer/context, and verdict in v1.1 review.json, bound to the exact claim ID,
revision, claim snapshot and its proof. The work summary need not be that proof. Disclose prior exposure; use
self_check when the independent read order was not respected. Verdicts are
reject, uncertain, or survives_current_attacks, never proof by critique alone.
A substantive repair requires review of the new candidate. Do not update
canonical state. No permanent Critic task or return-notification loop is needed.
