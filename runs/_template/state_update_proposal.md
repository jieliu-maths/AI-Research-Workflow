# Proposed canonical change

Provisional until explicit human approval. Create this file only when a formal
change is requested; link the proposal from the session work instead of copying
its derivation.

## Exact before and proposed after

Include claim status, hypotheses, necessary dependencies, route, notation, and
manuscript effects where relevant. Preserve distinct epistemic statuses.

## Frozen evidence and independent review

Link the exact statement, proof, derivation roots, and independent review of the
same claim/proof revision, matched to the intended target. Identify every
changed claim and its versioned prerequisite bindings; resolve stale evidence
before promotion. Scope a candidate failure separately from route rejection.

## Remaining gaps and owner decision

Record the exact approval reference only after the owner approves. No automatic
promotion follows a review verdict or successful consistency validation.
