## Required work product

For a candidate task, perform the preflight before the long derivation:

1. prove definition, continuity, and comparison in `canonical_topology`;
2. derive dimension-dependent adjoints, commutators, and boundary terms;
3. compute the first nonzero variation and compare its scale with the desired
   downstream margin;
4. quotient the complete tangent covector by `quotient_dependencies`;
5. run every named sign, remote, low-frequency, concentration, symmetry, and
   limiting-parameter test;
6. display cutoff speed, annular, exterior, primitive-boundary, and tail terms;
7. distinguish the raw formula, any repaired formula, the excluded class, and
   untested alternatives.

If a preflight test is fatal, stop there unless the frozen question explicitly
requires the repaired candidate to be tested as well.
